EVENT_0 

None.

EVENT_FORCEREDRAW 

Force a complete redraw.

EVENT_ANIMATE 

Animate document.

EVENT_NOEXPRESSION 

Do not execute expressions.

EVENT_GLHACK 

Private.

EVENT_CAMERAEXPRESSION 

If set (and not EVENT_NOEXPRESSION), the event will only update camera dependent expressions (for faster speed).

EVENT_ENQUEUE_REDRAW 

Do not stop the current redraw if DrawViews() is running at the moment. In that case enqueue the redraw after the current draw is done.
